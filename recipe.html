California Roll

The California Roll is one of the most popular types of sushi. It was created in the early 1970s to introduce North Americans to sushi. The exact inventor of the California Roll is unknown, but many people believe it was invented be Hidekazu Tojo in Vancouver, Canada. He used ingredients like imitation crab, avocado, and cucumber because customers were more familiar with those items. The California Roll has become very successful and popular all around the world.
